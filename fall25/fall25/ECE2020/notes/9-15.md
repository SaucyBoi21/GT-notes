## Mixed Logic & Number Systems
### Drawing Mixed Logic Diagrams
- We will utilize AND and OR gates, drawing a vertical bar for each negation
- Don't use inverters just yet - start with the smallest operations and build up towards the overall diagram
- In CMOS, NANDs and NORs use fewer switches than the positive counterparts
	- So we will convert all the AND and OR gates into NAND and NOR gates
	- From here, pair the bubbles created by the previous step
- Use DeMorgan's to change between NAND and NOR gates
### Number Systems - Radix R Representation
- Decimal number expansion (base 10) - the number system we are most used to, simply as we have 10 fingers
- You can easily convert between binary and hexadecimal by converting 4-bit quantities to hex
- To convert between base powers of two, utilize binary as an intermediate step
- For binary to decimal conversion, use mod 2 operations for the whole number portion
- Then multiply by 2 and keep the remainder, storing the whole number generated at each step
