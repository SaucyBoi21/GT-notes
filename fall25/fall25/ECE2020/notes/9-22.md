## Binary Operations
- When both numbers share a sign, simply add the magnitudes and then the sign will stay the same
- For subtraction, we will just subtract the numbers and the higher magnitude number will keep the sign
- Subtraction of signed numbers is best done with the 2's complement of the numbers
## Combinational Logic
- The outputs at any time are determined completely by the current inputs of the logical systems
- Unlike sequential logic, where the outputs are determined by both current inputs and the state (memory) of the system
### Decoders
- Standard decoders are and $n:m$ line decoder, where $m \leq 2^n$
- All outputs are low except for the one corresponding to the binary value of the input
