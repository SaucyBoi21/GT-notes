## Conditional Probability

 $$P(A / B) = \dfrac{A\cap B}{B}$$
 $$P(A\cap B) = P(B)P(A/B)$$
### Independent Random Events
 $$P(A \cap B) = P(A)P(B)$$
 Neither A nor B are dependent on one another, which means that they are mutually unrelated
 $$ A \implies A^c $$
$$ B \implies B^c$$
If A and B are independent, then $\neg$A and $\neg$B must also be independent
